# FireGuard Demo Policies

## Human approval

Resident alerts, evacuation orders, shelter-in-place instructions, road closure instructions, and dispatch movement require incident commander approval before execution.

## Shelter in place

If all self-evacuation routes cross active fire risk or known full closures, recommend temporary shelter-in-place and dispatch-assisted evacuation rather than blind evacuation.

## Data freshness

If fire, road, weather, or shelter data is stale, reduce confidence and present the uncertainty in the approval queue.
